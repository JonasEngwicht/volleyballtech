Passing is the skill every rally starts with, and you can practice it alone. A wall returns the ball the same way every time, which makes it the most patient training partner you will ever have.

## What you need

- A volleyball
- A solid, flat wall with a few yards of space in front of it

## Setup

Stand about six to eight feet from the wall. Pick a target on the wall a little above net height, or mark one with tape.

## How to do it

1. **Get in your ready position.** Feet a bit wider than your shoulders, knees bent, weight on the balls of your feet.
2. **Build your platform.** Put one hand in the other, thumbs side by side and pointing down, arms straight.
3. **Toss the ball against the wall** and let it drop to waist height.
4. **Pass it back to your target.** Contact the ball on your forearms, just above the wrists. Push with your legs, not with an arm swing.
5. **Keep it going.** Count how many passes you can make in a row.

## Progression

- **Level 1:** Toss, pass, catch. Reset each time.
- **Level 2:** Continuous passes. Aim for 10, then 25 in a row.
- **Level 3:** Take a step back after every five passes.
- **Level 4:** Alternate one forearm pass and one overhead pass.

## What to watch for

- **Quiet arms.** Your platform should barely move. The power comes from your legs.
- **Move your feet.** Get behind the ball instead of reaching sideways.
- **Angle the platform.** Your arms point where the ball should go.

## Common mistakes

- Bending the elbows at contact
- Swinging the arms up above the shoulders
- Standing straight-legged

Ten focused minutes a few times a week are better than one long session.
