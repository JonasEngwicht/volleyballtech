You don't need a setter or a net to improve your arm swing. In wall spiking, you hit the ball down into the floor so it bounces up off a wall and comes back to you. Then you hit it again. It builds a fast, high arm swing, a strong wrist snap and good contact, and every hitter can do it alone.

![Wall spiking drill side view: the player stands 10 to 15 feet from a wall, hits the ball down into the floor a few feet in front of the wall, the ball bounces up off the wall and comes back to the player, who hits again](/diagrams/wall-spiking.svg)

## What you need

- A volleyball
- A solid wall and a hard floor (a gym, garage or driveway)

## Setup

Stand about **10 to 15 feet** from the wall. Beginners can start closer.

## How to do it

1. **Toss and hit.** Toss the ball up in front of your hitting shoulder and hit it down so it lands on the floor **3 to 5 feet in front of the wall**.
2. **Let it bounce.** The ball hits the floor, bounces into the wall and pops back up toward you.
3. **Reach and hit again.** Draw your elbow back high, reach up and hit the ball at full reach as it comes back.
4. **Keep it going.** Count how many hits in a row you can make.

## The arm swing checklist

- **Elbow high and back** before you swing, with your hand near your ear.
- **Lead with the elbow,** then whip the forearm and hand through.
- **Contact in front of your hitting shoulder,** at full reach.
- **Open, firm hand** covering the top back of the ball.
- **Snap the wrist** so the ball comes off with topspin.
- **Follow through** down and across your body.

## Progression

- **Level 1:** Toss, hit once into the floor, catch the rebound. Reset.
- **Level 2:** Continuous hits. Aim for 10 in a row, then 25.
- **Level 3:** Take a step back every 10 hits for more power.
- **Level 4:** Aim at targets on the floor (tape squares) to work on accuracy.
- **Level 5:** Alternate hard hits and controlled roll shots.

## What to watch for

- **The ball should spin forward.** If it floats or spins sideways, your hand isn't on top of the ball.
- **Your non-hitting arm** should rise up in front of you, then pull down as you swing.
- **Rotate your shoulders.** Power comes from the core and shoulder rotation, not just the arm.

## Common mistakes

- **Dropping the elbow,** which makes you push the ball.
- **Hitting with a stiff, straight arm.** Bend at the elbow and whip it through.
- **Hitting behind your head.** The ball lands too close to you and the rebound goes over your head.
- **Too much too soon.** Start at 70 percent effort. Your shoulder needs time to adapt. Stop if it hurts.
