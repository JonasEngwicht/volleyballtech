The butterfly is one of the most popular team drills in volleyball. Servers serve, passers pass to a target at the net, and everyone follows their ball to the next station. It gives a whole team lots of serves and passes in a short time, and it runs on both sides of the net at once.

![Butterfly drill from above: a server line behind each end line serves down the line to a passer in left back, who passes to a target at the net. Players follow their ball: server becomes passer, passer becomes target, target joins the server line on the other side](/diagrams/butterfly-drill.svg)

## What you need

- A full court with a net
- 6 to 12 players (more players means longer lines)
- 4 to 6 volleyballs

## Setup

The drill runs the same way on both sides of the court:

- **Server line (S):** behind the end line in the right-back corner (zone 1), each player with a ball.
- **Passer (P):** one player in the left-back position (zone 5) on the other side of the net.
- **Target (T):** one player at the net in the setter's spot, between middle front and right front.

Because the server on each side serves down the line to the passer on the other side, the two halves mirror each other.

## How to do it

1. **Serve.** The first server serves down the line to the passer on the other side.
2. **Pass.** The passer passes the serve to the target at the net.
3. **Catch.** The target catches the pass.
4. **Follow your ball.** Everyone moves one station:
   - The **server** follows the serve around the net post to become the next **passer** on the other side.
   - The **passer** moves up to become the **target**.
   - The **target** takes the ball and jogs to the **server line** on the other side.
5. Both sides run at the same time, so the next serve comes quickly.

## Rotation in one line

**Server → passer → target → server line.** "Follow your ball" is the only rule players need to remember.

## Ways to score it

- **Team goal:** Count good passes (ones the target catches without moving more than a step). Stop when the team reaches 20, or set a time limit and try to beat your record.
- **Passing rating:** The target calls a score for each pass: 3 = perfect, 2 = setter has to move, 1 = only a free ball possible, 0 = ace or overpass.
- **Server accuracy:** Servers must hit the passer's zone. A missed serve costs the team a point.

## Variations

- **Switch sides:** Serve from zone 5 to zone 1 on the other side. Passers practice from the right back.
- **Add a setter:** The target becomes a setter who sets to a hitter or catcher on the left side.
- **Harder serves:** As the team improves, have servers serve tougher, aiming at seams and short zones.
- **Youth version:** Serve from inside the end line, or toss instead of serving.

## Coaching points

- **Passers:** Get your feet behind the ball and angle your platform to the target. See [how to pass a volleyball](/learn/how-to-pass-a-volleyball/).
- **Servers:** Serve to the passer, not just over the net. See [how to serve a volleyball](/learn/how-to-serve-a-volleyball/).
- **Targets:** Stand still and give a clear target with hands up. Don't chase bad passes; the passer needs honest feedback.
- **Everyone:** Hustle between stations. The drill stalls when someone walks.

## Common mistakes

- Running the drill with only one ball, so players stand around.
- Servers serving too hard, too early, so passers never get a rhythm.
- Passers reaching to the side instead of moving their feet.
- Forgetting the rotation. Say "follow your ball" out loud before you start.
