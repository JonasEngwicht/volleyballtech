Clean setting comes from hundreds of repetitions with the right hand shape. A wall lets you get those repetitions in minutes.

## What you need

- A volleyball
- A high, flat wall

## Setup

Stand about two to three feet from the wall. Pick a spot above your head as a target.

## Hand position

1. Raise your hands above your forehead.
2. Shape them like you are holding the ball: thumbs and index fingers form a loose triangle.
3. Spread your fingers. The ball touches the finger pads, not the palms.
4. Keep your elbows out and slightly forward.

## How to do it

1. **Short wall sets (2 minutes).** Stand close and set the ball against the wall in quick, small touches. Stay light and fast.
2. **Medium sets (3 minutes).** Take two steps back. Set higher, and use your legs: bend, then extend through the ball.
3. **Set, clap, set (2 minutes).** Clap once between touches. It forces you to rebuild your hand shape each time.
4. **Target sets (3 minutes).** Mark a square on the wall and count how many of 20 sets land inside it.

## What to watch for

- **Take the ball above your forehead.** If you could not head it, you are not under it.
- **Even hands.** Both hands leave the ball at the same moment. Uneven release causes spin.
- **Finish tall.** Extend your arms and follow through toward the target.

## Make it harder

- Set while sitting or kneeling to isolate your hands and wrists.
- Alternate one low set and one high set.
- Move sideways along the wall as you set.

If your wrists or fingers get sore, stop and rest. Soreness usually means the ball is hitting your palms or you are doing too many repetitions too soon.
