Good volleyball knee pads keep you from thinking about the floor. That makes you more willing to dive for a ball. Below are three pads we recommend, and who each one suits.

**How we chose:** These picks are based on manufacturer specs, retailer data and published buyer reviews. We have not worn them ourselves.

## The short answer

- **Best all-round pad:** [Mizuno LR6](/gear/knee-pads/mizuno-lr6-knee-pads/): slim, cheap and popular
- **Best for diving defenders:** [Mizuno Team](/gear/knee-pads/mizuno-team-knee-pads/): separate foam sections that cover the sides of the knee
- **Best for kids:** [ASICS Gel-Rally Youth](/gear/knee-pads/asics-gel-rally-youth-knee-pads/): youth sizing with GEL cushioning

## Mizuno LR6: the safe choice

A 6-inch, no-fold sleeve with padding in the impact zone. It is light and stays out of the way when you jump. If you are unsure which pad to buy, start here.

**Skip it if** you dive on almost every rally and want a thicker pad.

## Mizuno Team: more coverage for defenders

The foam is split into sections that wrap around the knee, so the sides of the joint are covered as well as the front. It suits liberos, defensive specialists and beginners who fall often.

**Skip it if** you want the slimmest pad possible.

## ASICS Gel-Rally Youth: a first pad for kids

Youth sizing keeps the pad where it belongs on a small leg, and the GEL cushioning softens knee-first landings. Check the size chart and re-measure each season.
