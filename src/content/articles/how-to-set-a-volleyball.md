To set a volleyball, move under the ball, raise your hands above your forehead with your thumbs and index fingers forming a triangle, contact the ball with the pads of all ten fingers, and extend your arms and legs toward your target in one smooth motion. The ball should leave your hands quickly, without spin.

Setting is the second touch in the classic pass-set-hit pattern. A good set gives your hitter the best possible chance to score.

![Four phases of an overhead volleyball set: move under the ball, hands up early forming a window above the forehead, catch-like contact on the finger pads, extend arms and legs toward the target](/diagrams/volleyball-setting-steps.svg)

## Step 1: Get to the ball early

Move so the ball will come down **just above your forehead**. Stop moving before it arrives. Setters who are still moving at contact give inconsistent sets.

**Face your target.** For a front set to the left-side hitter, your shoulders and hips face that direction. Many coaches teach setters to have the right foot slightly in front of the left so the body is turned toward the left side.

## Step 2: Hands up, early

- Raise both hands above your forehead **before** the ball arrives.
- Spread your fingers wide and curve them as if holding the ball.
- Your thumbs and index fingers form a **triangle or "window"**. You should be able to look at the ball through it.
- Thumbs point back toward your eyebrows, not at the ball.
- Elbows out, at about shoulder width.

## Step 3: Contact with the finger pads

- The ball touches the **pads of your fingers and thumbs**, not your palms.
- Most of the control comes from your thumbs, index and middle fingers. Ring and pinky fingers help guide the ball.
- Let the ball sink slightly into your hands, then push it out right away. It's a quick, soft contact, not a catch.
- Both hands must touch the ball at the same time. Uneven hands cause spin and double contacts.

## Step 4: Extend toward the target

Push the ball out by extending your **legs, then arms** in one motion, finishing with your arms straight and your hands pointing at the target. Think about setting from your legs: the farther the set, the more leg push you need.

## The back set

To set behind you to the right-side hitter:

1. Get under the ball the same way as for a front set, so the hitter and blockers can't guess.
2. Let the ball come slightly further over your forehead.
3. Arch your back and push your hands **up and back** over your head.
4. Keep your eyes on the ball.

## Setting rules: lifts and double contacts

Referees watch setters closely.

- **Double contact:** the ball touches one hand, then the other, or spins visibly off uneven hands. It's legal on the first team contact, but a fault when setting the second ball.
- **Lift or carry:** the ball comes to rest in your hands, or you push it for too long. Contact must be quick.

The best way to avoid both is clean, symmetrical hands and quick contact. In beach volleyball, setting is judged even more strictly. See [beach volleyball rules](/learn/beach-volleyball-rules/).

## Common setting mistakes

- **Hands too low.** Setting at chest level means pushing instead of setting. Get under the ball.
- **Palms touching the ball.** That's a slap, not a set. Keep a cup shape.
- **Thumbs pointing at the ball.** It jams your thumbs and makes the set low. Point them back.
- **Arms only.** Without leg push, sets fall short.
- **Watching the hitter.** Watch the ball until it leaves your hands.

## When to set and when to bump

Use your hands whenever the ball is high and slow enough to get under it. When the pass is low or fast, bump-set it: a high, accurate forearm pass to the hitter is better than a risky hand set. See [how to pass a volleyball](/learn/how-to-pass-a-volleyball/).

## How to practice setting

- **[Wall setting](/drills/wall-setting/):** set against a wall to build quick, clean hands.
- **Set to yourself:** set the ball straight up, 2–3 feet above your head, 50 times in a row. Then make the sets higher.
- **Use a heavier ball.** A weighted setting ball, like the [Tachikara "The Setter"](/gear/training-equipment/tachikara-the-setter-training-volleyball/), strengthens fingers and wrists.
- **Set into a target.** A setting trainer like the [Tandem Set-Rite](/gear/training-equipment/tandem-set-rite-setting-trainer/) gives you instant feedback on accuracy.
