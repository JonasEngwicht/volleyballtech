Landing on another player's foot under the net is a common way to roll an ankle in volleyball. That is why many players wear ankle braces. This guide explains the main types and how to choose.

*We are not medical professionals. If you have an injury or ongoing pain, ask a doctor or physical therapist before choosing a brace.*

## Why players wear ankle braces

There are two main reasons:

- **Prevention:** A brace limits how far the ankle can roll, which helps protect healthy ankles that tend to turn.
- **Return to play:** After a sprain, a brace can add support while the ankle regains strength. A professional should advise on this.

## Types of volleyball ankle braces

| Type | How it works | Support | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sleeve | Compression only | Light | Not covered |
| Lace-up with straps | Laces plus figure-6 or figure-8 straps | Moderate to high | [McDavid 195](/gear/ankle-braces/mcdavid-195-ankle-brace/), [ASO](/gear/ankle-braces/aso-ankle-stabilizer/) |
| Rigid or hinged | Plastic side guards plus straps | Strongest | [Zamst A2-DX](/gear/ankle-braces/zamst-a2-dx-ankle-brace/) |

## Lace-up braces

Most volleyball players choose lace-up braces. They are adjustable, fit in court shoes and copy the effect of ankle tape. They take a few tries to put on correctly.

## Rigid braces

These add plastic guards on the sides. They give the most control and are often used by players with repeated sprains. They cost more and can feel bulky.

## Sleeves

Compression sleeves feel good and keep the ankle warm, but they do not stop it from rolling. They are not a substitute for a brace.

## How to choose

1. **Decide why you need it.** Prevention and recovery are different jobs.
2. **Measure your ankle.** Sizes are based on ankle circumference. Follow the maker's chart.
3. **Check shoe fit.** Try the brace with your court shoes. Some braces need a roomier shoe.
4. **Practice putting it on.** A brace you cannot lace in the locker room will not get used.

## Care

Rinse or hand wash the brace and air-dry it. Check the laces and straps for wear.
