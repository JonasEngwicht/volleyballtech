Volleyball has its own language. At your first practice or match you'll hear "pipe", "pancake", "tool" and "side-out" within minutes. This glossary explains the most common volleyball terms in plain English, grouped by topic so you can find them quickly.

## Basic skills and contacts

- **Pass:** Any controlled first contact that sends the ball to the setter. Usually a forearm pass. See [how to pass a volleyball](/learn/how-to-pass-a-volleyball/).
- **Bump:** Another word for a forearm pass, with the hands joined and the ball played off the forearms.
- **Platform:** The flat surface your forearms form when you bump the ball.
- **Set:** The second contact, usually overhead with the fingertips, placing the ball for a hitter. See [how to set a volleyball](/learn/how-to-set-a-volleyball/).
- **Bump set:** A set made with the forearms instead of the hands.
- **Attack / hit / spike:** An attempt to send the ball into the opponent's court to win the point, usually with a jump. See [how to spike a volleyball](/learn/how-to-spike-a-volleyball/).
- **Block:** One or more front-row players jumping at the net to stop or deflect an attack. See [how to block in volleyball](/learn/how-to-block-in-volleyball/).
- **Dig:** Passing a hard-driven attack, keeping the ball in play.
- **Serve:** The overhand or underhand hit that starts every rally. See [how to serve a volleyball](/learn/how-to-serve-a-volleyball/).
- **Serve receive:** Passing the opponent's serve.
- **Free ball:** An easy ball sent over the net without an attack, usually with a pass or set. Teams often shout "free!"
- **Down ball:** A ball hit over the net from a standing position, without jumping.
- **Overpass:** A pass that goes over the net by accident, often an easy point for the opponent.
- **Pancake:** A defensive save where a player slides a flat hand along the floor and lets the ball bounce off the back of it.
- **Sprawl:** A low dive in which the player extends and lands on the floor to reach a ball.
- **Shank:** A badly mishit pass that flies off in the wrong direction.

## Positions

Read [volleyball positions explained](/guides/volleyball-positions-explained/) for details on each role.

- **Setter:** The player who takes the second contact and runs the offense.
- **Outside hitter (OH) / left side:** The main attacker on the left side of the court. Also passes serve.
- **Opposite (OPP) / right side:** The attacker on the right side, opposite the setter in the rotation.
- **Middle blocker (MB) / middle:** Blocks in the middle of the net and hits fast sets.
- **Libero:** A back-row defensive specialist in a different colored jersey. May not attack above the net or block.
- **Defensive specialist (DS):** A back-row substitute for passing and defense who follows normal substitution rules.
- **Front row:** The three players in zones 2, 3 and 4, close to the net.
- **Back row:** The three players in zones 1, 5 and 6.

## Attacks and sets

- **Kill:** An attack that directly scores a point.
- **Tip / dink:** A soft, open-handed push of the ball over or around the block. Not allowed in beach volleyball.
- **Roll shot:** A soft attack with topspin that arcs over the block into open space.
- **Tool / wipe:** Hitting the ball off the blocker's hands so it lands out of bounds. The attacking team scores.
- **Line shot:** An attack straight down the sideline.
- **Cross-court / angle:** An attack diagonally across the court.
- **Cut shot:** A sharp-angled attack, close to the net.
- **Back-row attack:** An attack by a back-row player who takes off behind the attack line.
- **Pipe:** A back-row attack from the middle back position.
- **Quick set / 1 ball:** A low, fast set to the middle blocker, who is already in the air.
- **Slide:** An attack in which the hitter (usually the middle) runs along the net and takes off from one foot.
- **Back set:** A set that goes behind the setter, usually to the right side.
- **Shoot set:** A low, fast set to the outside or right side.
- **Dump:** The setter attacking the second ball over the net instead of setting.
- **Joust:** When two opposing players push the ball at the same time above the net.
- **Hitting error:** An attack that goes out, into the net, or is blocked down.

## Rules and faults

Full explanations in our [volleyball rules guide](/learn/volleyball-rules/).

- **Rally scoring:** Every rally wins a point, no matter which team served.
- **Side-out:** Winning the rally when the other team serves. You get the point and the serve.
- **Rotation:** Moving one position clockwise after a side-out. See [volleyball rotation explained](/learn/volleyball-rotation/).
- **Overlap / rotation fault:** Players out of their correct order at the moment of the serve.
- **Lift / carry:** A fault in which the ball comes to rest in a player's hands.
- **Double contact / double hit:** The ball touches a player twice in a row, or hits two body parts in separate actions. Allowed only on the first team contact.
- **Four hits:** A team touches the ball more than three times. A block doesn't count indoors.
- **Net violation:** Touching the net between the antennas while playing the ball.
- **Center line violation:** A foot crossing completely over the center line into the opponent's court.
- **Foot fault:** Stepping on or over the end line before contact on the serve.
- **Back-row violation:** A back-row player attacking a ball above the net from in front of the attack line.
- **Reaching over:** Playing the ball on the opponent's side before they've attacked.
- **Let serve:** A serve that touches the net and still goes over. It is legal and stays in play.
- **Antenna:** The flexible rods on top of the net that mark the side limits. A ball that touches them is out.
- **Replay:** A rally replayed without a point, for example after a double fault.

## Scoring and statistics

- **Ace:** A serve that scores directly, without being returned.
- **Service error:** A serve into the net or out of bounds.
- **Assist:** A set (or pass) that leads directly to a kill.
- **Hitting percentage:** (Kills minus errors) divided by total attacks. A .300 hitting percentage is very good.
- **Block solo / block assist:** A block by one player, or by two or three players together.
- **Reception error:** A serve-receive pass that directly loses the point.
- **Set (as in a game):** One game within a match, played to 25 (or 15 in a deciding set). See [volleyball scoring](/learn/volleyball-scoring/).
- **Match:** Usually best of three or best of five sets.
- **Run:** Several points in a row by one team.

## Court and equipment

- **Attack line / 10-foot line:** The line 3 meters (about 10 feet) from the net. See [volleyball court dimensions](/learn/volleyball-court-dimensions/).
- **End line / base line:** The line at the back of the court. The server stands behind it.
- **Sideline:** The lines along the long sides of the court.
- **Free zone:** The open area around the court where players can still play the ball.
- **Zones 1–6:** The six positions on each side, numbered from the server's spot counter-clockwise.
- **Net height:** 7 ft 11⅝ in for men, 7 ft 4⅛ in for women. See [volleyball net height](/learn/volleyball-net-height/).
- **Knee pads:** Padded sleeves that protect the knees when diving. See our [knee pads](/gear/knee-pads/).
- **Court shoes:** Shoes with gum rubber soles for grip on indoor floors. See [volleyball shoes](/gear/shoes/).
- **Ankle brace:** A lace-up or hinged support to help prevent sprains. See [ankle braces](/gear/ankle-braces/).

## Slang you'll hear

- **Six pack / facial:** Getting hit in the face by an attack. It happens.
- **Roof:** A stuff block straight down.
- **Husband and wife:** When a ball drops between two players who each expect the other to take it.
- **Mine! / Got it!:** What you shout when you take the ball. Call it early and loudly.
- **Free ball!:** Called when an easy ball is coming over.
- **Down ball!:** Called when the opponent will hit without jumping, so blockers stay down.
