Volleyball tech measures and records your game. It does not train you, but the right tool can show you what to work on. Here are three we think are worth a look.

**How we chose:** These picks are based on manufacturer information, App Store listings and published reviews. We have not tested them ourselves.

## The short answer

- **Best jump tracker:** [VERT jump monitor](/gear/tech/vert-jump-monitor/)
- **Best stats app for coaches:** [SoloStats 123](/gear/tech/solostats-123/)
- **Best auto-tracking camera:** [Veo Cam 3](/gear/tech/veo-cam-3/), for programs that can pay for it

## Best volleyball tech at a glance

| Product | Type | Best for | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [VERT](/gear/tech/vert-jump-monitor/) | Jump tracker | Hitters, coaches | Device plus optional app plans |
| [SoloStats 123](/gear/tech/solostats-123/) | Stats app | Coaches, parents | Free with in-app purchases |
| [Veo Cam 3](/gear/tech/veo-cam-3/) | Auto-tracking camera | Clubs and schools | Hardware plus yearly subscription |

## VERT

A small sensor on your waist that counts jumps and estimates height. It is most useful for tracking jump load over a week.

## SoloStats 123

A free app that lets one person record a match. Start with serves and passes, then add more.

## Veo Cam 3

Records the whole court and builds a tracked view. It is pricey, and a phone on a tripod gives you much of the benefit.

## More volleyball tech worth a look

- **[XbotGo Chameleon](/gear/tech/xbotgo-chameleon/):** a motorized mount that turns your phone into an auto-tracking camera, with no subscription. The budget alternative to a dedicated camera.
- **[Pocket Radar Ball Coach](/gear/tech/pocket-radar-ball-coach/):** a pocket radar gun that shows serve and spike speed, for players and coaches who train with numbers.
