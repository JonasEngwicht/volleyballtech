The [McDavid 195](/gear/ankle-braces/mcdavid-195-ankle-brace/) and the [ASO Ankle Stabilizer](/gear/ankle-braces/aso-ankle-stabilizer/) are two of the best-known lace-up braces. Both use straps to limit ankle rolling. They differ in materials, feel and price.

## Side by side

| | McDavid 195 | ASO Ankle Stabilizer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Type | Lace-up, figure-6 straps | Lace-up, figure-8 straps |
| Material | Single-layer nylon/polyester | Ballistic nylon |
| Feel | Light, thin | Sturdier, more structure |
| Fits either foot | Yes | Yes |
| Typical price | About $30–$45 | Varies by version |
| Sizes | Small to extra large | Extra small to 3X-large |
| Best for | Light to moderate support | Firm support, history of sprains |

*Based on manufacturer and retailer information. We have not worn either brace.*

## Support

Both are lace-up braces with straps that cross the ankle. The ASO's heavier fabric gives more structure, while the McDavid is thinner and more flexible.

## Fit in your shoes

The McDavid is thin and usually fits most court shoes. The ASO is designed to be low profile as well. If your shoes are snug, try on both with the brace.

## Price

The McDavid is generally inexpensive. ASO prices depend on the version, so compare the exact model you want.

## Which one should you buy?

- **Choose the McDavid 195** if you want light protection and an easy fit.
- **Choose the ASO** if you have rolled your ankle before and want sturdier support.
- **Look at a rigid brace** like the [Zamst A2-DX](/gear/ankle-braces/zamst-a2-dx-ankle-brace/) if you have chronic instability.
