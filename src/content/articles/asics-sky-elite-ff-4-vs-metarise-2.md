ASICS puts both the [Sky Elite FF 4](/gear/shoes/asics-sky-elite-ff-4/) and the [Metarise 2](/gear/shoes/asics-metarise-2/) in the group of shoes it recommends for a "higher" playing style. They go about it in different ways.

*Based on ASICS product information, checked November 2026. We have not worn either shoe.*

## How they differ on the court

**Sky Elite FF 4.** The newer shoe, launched in June 2026. It relies on its foam: soft when you land, springy when you push off. It works for a straight-up block jump as well as an approach jump, so it also suits middle blockers.

**Metarise 2.** Built around the approach. The curved toe lets you roll forward into your plant, and the plate spreads pressure across the forefoot as you take off. That design is aimed at hitters who convert a fast run-up into height.

## Choose the Sky Elite FF 4 if…

- you are a middle blocker or play several front-row positions
- you want softer landings above all
- you want a mid-top option
- you are still refining your approach

## Choose the Metarise 2 if…

- you are an outside or opposite hitter with a fast, repeatable approach
- you train often enough to justify the price
- you like a snug, sock-like upper

## Our take

For most players the Sky Elite FF 4 is the better choice. It is more versatile, comes in more sizes and cuts, and costs less. The Metarise 2 is a tool for a specific job. If you are not sure you need it, you probably do not.
