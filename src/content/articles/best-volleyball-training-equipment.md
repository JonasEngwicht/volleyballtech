## Start with free options

A wall costs nothing. Try the [wall passing](/drills/wall-passing/) and [wall setting](/drills/wall-setting/) drills before you buy anything.

## Rebounder net

It sends the ball back so you can practice alone. Choose one that folds flat and has an adjustable angle.

## Setting trainers

Straps with a palm knob help you stop palming the ball. A weighted ball builds strength. Use both sparingly.

## Portable net

A regulation net turns a backyard or park into a court. It is for fun and general practice rather than serious training.

## What to skip

Gadgets that promise a higher jump. Training and rest do that. See [how to jump higher for volleyball](/guides/how-to-jump-higher-for-volleyball/).
