If your shoes have started to slide, do not replace them yet. Most of the time the rubber is fine and the problem is a thin layer of dust.

## Quick fix during a match

- Wipe each sole with a slightly damp towel during timeouts.
- Wiping your soles with your hand works in a pinch, which is why you see players do it between rallies.
- Some gyms have sticky mats at the court entrance. Use them.

## How to clean volleyball shoes at home

1. **Knock off loose dirt.** Bang the soles together outside.
2. **Scrub the outsole.** Use an old toothbrush, warm water and a drop of dish soap. Work the dust out of the grooves.
3. **Wipe and dry.** Wipe with a clean damp cloth, then let the soles air-dry completely.
4. **Clean the upper.** A damp cloth and mild soap are enough. Scrub gently on mesh.
5. **Deal with the inside.** Take the insoles out and let everything air out.

Clean the soles every week or two if you play regularly, and more often in dusty gyms.

## What not to do

- **No washing machine.** It can loosen glue and deform the shoe.
- **No dryer, heater or direct sun.** Heat hardens rubber and breaks down foam and glue.
- **No harsh cleaners or solvents.** They can damage the rubber.
- **No sticky sprays unless your league allows them.** Some leave residue on the floor.

## How to keep volleyball shoes grippy

- **Wear them only indoors.** This is the big one. Change into them at the gym.
- **Carry them in a bag,** not on your feet from the car.
- **Let them dry after every session.** Loosen the laces, pull the tongue forward and take the insoles out.
- **Stuff them with newspaper** if they are soaked with sweat.

## Getting rid of smell

- Dry them fully after each use.
- Rotate insoles or wash them by hand.
- Wear clean, moisture-wicking socks.
- A little baking soda left overnight helps. Shake it out before you play.

## When cleaning is not enough

It is time for a new pair when:

- the tread pattern is worn smooth, especially under the ball of the foot
- the rubber feels hard and shiny even when clean
- the cushioning feels flat and landings feel harsher than before
