The [Gel-Rocket 12](/gear/shoes/asics-gel-rocket-12/) and the [Upcourt 6](/gear/shoes/asics-upcourt-6/) are the two ASICS shoes most beginners end up choosing between. They look similar and cost almost the same. The difference is in the midsole.

## Side by side

| | Gel-Rocket 12 | Upcourt 6 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| List price (Nov 2026) | About $80 | About $65 |
| Cushioning | PureGEL in the midsole | Basic EVA foam |
| Midfoot stability | TRUSSTIC support piece | Overlays in the upper only |
| Outsole | Rubber with flex grooves | Non-marking rubber |
| Wide version | Yes (men's sizes) | No |
| Kids' version | No | Yes (Upcourt 6 GS) |
| Best for | Weekly players, first club season | PE, occasional play, kids |

*Based on ASICS and retailer product information. We have not worn either shoe.*

## What the extra $15 buys

**Cushioning.** The Gel-Rocket adds a gel insert that absorbs some of the shock when you land. The Upcourt relies on plain foam.

**Stability.** The Gel-Rocket has a rigid piece under the arch that resists twisting. That helps when you stop hard and change direction.

Neither difference is dramatic. Both add up if you play regularly.

## Choose the Gel-Rocket 12 if…

- you play or train at least once a week
- you are an adult or a teen who has stopped growing quickly
- you have wide feet
- you play a front-row position and jump often

## Choose the Upcourt 6 if…

- you are buying for a child who will outgrow the shoe in a season
- you need indoor shoes for PE or occasional open gym
- you are not sure yet whether volleyball will stick

## Our take

For most new players the Gel-Rocket 12 is the better buy. The price gap is small and the shoe will keep up with you for longer. The Upcourt 6 makes sense when the shoe will be outgrown or rarely used.
