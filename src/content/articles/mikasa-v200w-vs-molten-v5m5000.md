The [Mikasa V200W](/gear/balls/mikasa-v200w/) and the [Molten V5M5000](/gear/balls/molten-v5m5000/) are two of the best-known indoor match balls. Both are FIVB approved, and both cost about the same. The real question is which one your league plays with.

## Side by side

| | Mikasa V200W | Molten V5M5000 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Use | Indoor | Indoor |
| Panels | 18 | 18 (hexagonal FLISTATEC pattern) |
| Cover | Microfiber composite, dimpled | Microfiber composite |
| Approvals | FIVB, Tokyo 2020 ball | FIVB, NORCECA |
| Typical price | About $80–$100 | About $90 |
| Best for | Mikasa leagues and clubs | Molten leagues and clubs |

*Based on manufacturer and retailer information. We have not played with either ball.*

## Design

Mikasa uses a dimpled surface for grip. Molten uses its FLISTATEC hexagonal pattern to steady the ball in flight.

## Price

They are close. Check current prices at your retailer, because both vary.

## Which one should you buy?

- **Buy the ball your league uses.** Practicing with the match ball is the main benefit.
- **If your league has no preference,** either is a solid choice, and a cheaper ball is enough for casual play.
