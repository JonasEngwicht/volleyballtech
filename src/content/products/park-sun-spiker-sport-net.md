## Who it is for

If you want to play at home or at the park, a portable net opens up the game for friends and family. The Spiker Sport is designed for recreational play and comes with the lines and stakes you need.

It works best on grass or sand. Outdoor surfaces are forgiving, but the stakes need soft ground.

## Who should skip it

- **Serious competitors.** Tournament nets are sturdier and tension better.
- **People who play indoors.** This is an outdoor system.

For solo practice, a [rebounder net](/gear/training-equipment/apex-volleyball-rebounder-net/) is more useful. See our [training equipment picks](/best/best-volleyball-training-equipment/).
