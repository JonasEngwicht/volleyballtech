## Who it is for

Zamst designs the A2-DX for athletes dealing with serious sprains and chronic instability. The plastic guards keep the ankle from rolling, and the X-straps hold the foot in place.

If you have rolled your ankle repeatedly and a lace-up brace did not feel secure enough, this is the type of brace to look at.

## Who should skip it

- **Players who only want light protection.** A lace-up like the [McDavid 195](/gear/ankle-braces/mcdavid-195-ankle-brace/) is cheaper, thinner and quicker to put on.
- **Anyone with a current injury who has not seen a professional.** Ask a doctor or physical therapist which brace fits your injury.

## Fitting tips

Take time to learn the straps at home before a match. Check that your court shoes still lace up comfortably over the brace.
