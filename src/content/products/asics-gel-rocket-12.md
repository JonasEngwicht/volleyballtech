## Who it is for

The Gel-Rocket has been the default answer to "what should I buy for my first season?" for years. The twelfth version keeps the recipe: a grippy indoor outsole, a bit of gel cushioning and a stable midfoot, at a price that does not hurt.

It is a good pick for new players, for teenagers whose feet are still growing and for anyone who plays once or twice a week.

## Who should skip it

- **Players who train several times a week and jump a lot.** Step up to a shoe with more forefoot cushioning, such as the [Mizuno Wave Momentum 3](/gear/shoes/mizuno-wave-momentum-3/).
- **Anyone who wants the cheapest option.** The [Upcourt 6](/gear/shoes/asics-upcourt-6/) costs less. Here is [how the two compare](/compare/asics-gel-rocket-12-vs-upcourt-6/).
