## Who it is for

If you have watched high school or college volleyball in the United States, you have seen this brace: a plastic stirrup on each side of the ankle, joined by a hinge, held on with one wide strap. A 2022 university thesis on bracing in volleyball named the T2 and the lace-up ASO as the two braces players use most.

The idea is simple. Most ankle sprains in volleyball happen at the net, when a player lands on someone else's foot and the ankle rolls outward. The stiff sides of the T2 resist that roll. The hinge lets the foot point up and down, which is the movement you need to jump, land and run.

The brace suits:

- **Middles and outside hitters**, who land in traffic at the net
- **Players coming back from a sprain**, if a doctor or athletic trainer agrees
- **Parents and coaches** who want solid protection that is quick to put on

## Who should skip it

- **Players who want a barely-there feel.** A lace-up such as the [McDavid 195](/gear/ankle-braces/mcdavid-195-ankle-brace/) or the [ASO Ankle Stabilizer](/gear/ankle-braces/aso-ankle-stabilizer/) is softer and slimmer.
- **Liberos and defensive specialists.** They land at the net less often, and many prefer a softer brace or none.
- **Anyone with an unusual injury.** A brace is not a treatment. Ask a professional first.

## Sizing and fit

The T2 is sized by shoe size, and the same brace goes on either foot. The inside is padded, and the pads and strap can be replaced with a kit when they wear out, which makes the brace last longer than one season.

Three tips from players who wear rigid braces:

1. **Wear a tall sock** under the brace so the plastic does not touch your skin.
2. **Loosen your laces all the way** before you put the shoe on, then tighten from the toes up.
3. **Give it a few practices.** A rigid brace feels strange at first.

Check that your shoes have room. We cover this in [how to choose volleyball ankle braces](/guides/how-to-choose-volleyball-ankle-braces/).

## How it compares

- **Against the [Active Ankle Eclipse II](/gear/ankle-braces/active-ankle-eclipse-ii/):** the Eclipse II is the same maker's top model, with a solid U-shaped shell and more straps. The T2 is simpler, cheaper and quicker to put on.
- **Against the [Ultra Zoom](/gear/ankle-braces/ultra-zoom-ankle-brace/):** the Ultra Zoom also has a hinge, but its shell is a flexible material that molds to your ankle with body heat. It costs more.

## Good to know

The maker says the current T2 uses a shell material that is ten times more durable than the one on earlier versions. If you buy a used or very old brace, you may be getting the older plastic.
