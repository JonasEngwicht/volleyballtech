## Who it is for

Auto-tracking cameras are for programs that want to film every match without paying a camera operator. Veo records the whole playing area and lets coaches review the match later.

For a club or school team that records often, it can save hours.

## Who should skip it

- **Individual players and families on a budget.** A phone on a tripod gives you most of the value for far less money.
- **Teams that cannot pay a yearly subscription.**

## A cheaper option first

A court is small enough for one camera to see all of it, so mounting a phone high behind the baseline works well. Our [volleyball tech guide](/guides/volleyball-tech-guide/) explains what to look for.
