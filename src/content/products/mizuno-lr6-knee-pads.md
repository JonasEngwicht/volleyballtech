## Who it is for

The LR6 is built for players who go to the floor sometimes, but not on every rally. Hitters, setters and middle blockers like it because it is low profile and does not get in the way when you jump or move.

If you are new to volleyball and still learning to dive safely, it is a sensible, cheap first pair.

## Who should skip it

- **Liberos and defensive specialists.** If you slide and dive all match, you will probably prefer a thicker pad. Compare the options in our [knee pad buying guide](/guides/how-to-choose-volleyball-knee-pads/).
- **Anyone between sizes.** Check the size chart. A pad that slides down protects nothing.

## How to wear it

Pull the pad on so the padded area sits over the front of your knee and the bone just below it. That is the area that meets the floor first when you drop to a knee.
