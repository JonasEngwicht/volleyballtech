## Who it is for

Mizuno sells several knee pads that look alike from a distance. The T10 Plus is the one with the longer sleeve.

A longer sleeve does two things. It grips more of your leg, so the pad is less likely to slide down. And it covers more skin, which helps on gym floors that give you burns when you slide. The pad itself is slim, and the foam is split into sections so it can bend when your knee bends.

It is a good pad for school teams and new players: easy to buy, easy to wear, and around $25 a pair.

## Who should skip it

- **Players at either end of the size range.** One adult size is supposed to fit knees from about 12 to 17.5 inches around. Buyer reviews include complaints that it is very tight, even on slim legs. If you are near the top of the range, pick a pad with real sizes, such as the [Mizuno LR6](/gear/knee-pads/mizuno-lr6-knee-pads/) or the [Nike Streak](/gear/knee-pads/nike-streak-knee-pads/).
- **Liberos and defensive specialists.** More foam and more side coverage, as on the [Mizuno Team](/gear/knee-pads/mizuno-team-knee-pads/), is worth the extra bulk.
- **Hitters who hate fabric behind the knee.** A short sleeve like the LR6 is cooler.

## Sizing and fit

Measure around the middle of your kneecap with your leg slightly bent. Compare that number with the range in the listing. If you are within an inch of the upper limit, expect a tight fit.

The youth version is a separate product with its own range. For a child who is between the two, the youth pad usually fits better, because a pad that is too big slides down during play.

## How it compares

- **Against the [Mizuno LR6](/gear/knee-pads/mizuno-lr6-knee-pads/):** same brand, same slim idea. The LR6 is shorter and comes in three sizes. The T10 Plus is longer and one-size.
- **Against the [ASICS Gel-Rally Youth](/gear/knee-pads/asics-gel-rally-youth-knee-pads/):** for kids, both are sensible first pads. The ASICS uses GEL cushioning; the Mizuno youth pad uses sectioned foam.
- **Against the [Nike Streak](/gear/knee-pads/nike-streak-knee-pads/):** the Nike costs a little more and offers four adult sizes.

## Good to know

"No-fold" means the top of the sleeve is finished so that it does not roll over on itself. Older pad designs had a folded cuff that bunched up behind the knee.

Knee pads are safety gear, but they only prevent bruises and floor burns. They do not support the joint. If your knee hurts when you play, see a doctor or physical therapist.
