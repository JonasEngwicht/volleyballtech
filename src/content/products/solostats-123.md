## Who it is for

A stats app records every serve, pass and attack, and the numbers help a coach decide what to practice. SoloStats is built so one person can keep up without an assistant.

It is a good first stats app for a coach, parent or team manager: free to try, with enough detail for a school or club season.

## Start simple

Do not track everything on day one. Begin with serves in, passing quality and kills, then add more once the habit sticks.

## Who should skip it

- **Players who just want to play.** Stats are a coaching tool.
- **Android users**, unless the app is available for your phone. Check the store listing.
