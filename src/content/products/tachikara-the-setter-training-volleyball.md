## Who it is for

Setting takes finger strength and soft hands. The Setter weighs far more than a normal ball, so a few minutes of controlled sets make the regular ball feel light afterward.

Teams use it as a short drill at the start of a setting session. It pairs well with the [wall setting drill](/drills/wall-setting/).

## How to use it

- Keep sets short, about five minutes at a time.
- Use it for setting only, not for serving or hitting.
- Stop if your fingers or wrists hurt.

## Who should skip it

- **Kids and first-time players.** Learn the technique with a normal ball first.
- **Anyone with finger or wrist pain.** Ask a coach or doctor before using a weighted ball.
