## Who it is for

You get more touches alone with a rebounder than without one. The net sends the ball back, so you can pass or set without a partner or a wall.

It works well for players who want extra repetition at home, and for parents looking for a gift that gets used.

## How to use it

- Set a low angle for passing, a steeper one for setting.
- Stand about a few steps away and keep the contact short.
- Use it with a wall drill like [wall passing](/drills/wall-passing/) for variety.

## Who should skip it

- **Players who already have a good wall.** A wall is free and returns the ball consistently.
- **Anyone short on storage space.** It folds flat, but it is still a 7 ft frame.
