## Who it is for

The Upcourt 6 is for players who need a proper non-marking indoor shoe and nothing more: PE class, a first rec league, a child trying volleyball for a season.

It does the basics. The rubber outsole grips indoor floors, the mesh upper breathes and the shoe is light on the foot.

## Who should skip it

- **Regular players.** If you train twice a week or more, the extra cushioning and support of the [Gel-Rocket 12](/gear/shoes/asics-gel-rocket-12/) is worth the small price difference.
- **Hitters and blockers.** Simple EVA foam is not much protection for repeated hard landings.
