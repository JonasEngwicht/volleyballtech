## Who it is for

There are two classic kinds of volleyball ankle brace. Lace-ups are soft and slim but work like a tight corset around the whole joint. Rigid braces protect more but feel like hard plastic, because they are.

The Ultra Zoom tries to sit between them. It has a hinge, like a rigid brace, so the foot can point up and down freely. But the shell is made of Performathane, a material that warms up against your skin and shapes itself to your ankle. After the break-in period the brace is molded to you.

The maker says the hinged-cuff design helps protect against both ordinary ankle sprains and high ankle sprains, the kind that happen above the joint.

It suits:

- **Hitters and blockers** who want hinged protection all season
- **Players who tried a hard brace and could not get comfortable**
- **Players who dislike lacing up** a soft brace before every session

## Who should skip it

- **Players on a budget.** Two braces cost well over $100. Two [McDavid 195](/gear/ankle-braces/mcdavid-195-ankle-brace/) lace-ups cost far less.
- **Athletes who need the most support possible.** The maker of the [Active Ankle Eclipse II](/gear/ankle-braces/active-ankle-eclipse-ii/) and Zamst with its [A2-DX](/gear/ankle-braces/zamst-a2-dx-ankle-brace/) both sell stiffer designs.
- **Players who want protection tomorrow.** Plan a few hours of wear first.

## Sizing and fit

There are two sizes, S/M and L/XL, chosen by shoe size. With so few sizes, the molding matters: the shell is meant to adapt to you.

For the break-in, the maker suggests 2 to 4 hours of wear. A practical way to do it is to wear the braces during two normal practices before you rely on them in a match. Expect them to feel stiff at the start of the first session and better by the end.

In size S/M the brace weighs about 5 ounces and stands about 7 inches tall. It is designed to fit inside a normal athletic shoe, but as with every brace, try it with the shoes you play in.

## How it compares

- **Against the [Active Ankle T2](/gear/ankle-braces/active-ankle-t2/):** both are hinged. The T2 is cheaper and uses hard shells. The Ultra Zoom is softer, molds to the ankle and costs more.
- **Against the [ASO Ankle Stabilizer](/gear/ankle-braces/aso-ankle-stabilizer/):** the ASO is the classic lace-up. It is cheaper and slimmer but has to be laced and strapped every time.
- **Against the [Zamst A2-DX](/gear/ankle-braces/zamst-a2-dx-ankle-brace/):** similar price. The Zamst is a strong-support brace for unstable ankles; the Ultra Zoom is aimed at prevention.
