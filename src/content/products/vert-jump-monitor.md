## Who it is for

A jump tracker answers two questions: how high do you jump, and how often? The second matters more for coaches, because big jump counts across a week can lead to overuse injuries.

VERT clips to your waistband and sends data to an app. Coaches can use the team system to see all players at once.

## Who should skip it

- **Beginners.** Spend the money on [shoes](/gear/shoes/) and coaching first.
- **Anyone who only wants a vertical number.** A wall and chalk are free. See [how to jump higher for volleyball](/guides/how-to-jump-higher-for-volleyball/).
