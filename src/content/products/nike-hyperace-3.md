## Who it is for

Nike describes the HyperAce 3 as a shoe for players who chase down every ball: quick, low and light. That makes it a natural fit for back-row players and a solid all-around choice for school and club teams.

It is also one of the easier shoes to match to team colors, which matters more to many teams than they like to admit.

## Who should skip it

- **Hitters who want the most lockdown and bounce.** The [HyperSet 2](/gear/shoes/nike-hyperset-2/) is Nike's step-up model.
- **Beginners who just need a first shoe.** You can spend much less on a [Gel-Rocket 12](/gear/shoes/asics-gel-rocket-12/).

## Sizing

The shoe is sold in unisex sizing, with both the men's and women's size printed on the box. Order by the size for your own gender and double-check the chart.
