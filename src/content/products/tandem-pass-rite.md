## Who it is for

Watch any group of beginners pass and you will see the same thing. The ball comes, the arms swing up to meet it, and the ball flies over the passer's head or into the ceiling.

Coaches say "keep your arms still" a hundred times a season. The Pass Rite says it without words. An elastic band runs from a small cuff on each wrist to each ankle. When the arms try to swing above a certain height, the band pulls back.

The player has to do what good passers do: get the feet to the ball, bend the knees, hold a steady platform and steer the ball with the angle of the arms.

It suits:

- **New players** in their first seasons
- **Older players with a stubborn arm swing**
- **Coaches** who want a tool that corrects without talking
- **Parents** helping a child practise at home

## Who should skip it

- **Players who already pass with quiet arms.** You need better serves to pass, not a band.
- **Players whose problem is footwork.** The band does not move your feet. Tandem makes a separate tool, the Base Rite, for the distance between the feet.
- **Anyone hoping for a shortcut.** It teaches a feeling. The repetitions are still up to you.

## How to use it well

1. **Set the length.** Stand in your passing posture with arms out. The band should be just tight. If it is slack, shorten it.
2. **Start with easy tosses.** Have a partner toss from a few steps away. Pass to a target, such as a person standing at the net.
3. **Take it off.** After ten minutes, pass without the band and try to keep the same feeling.

Our guide on [how to pass a volleyball](/learn/how-to-pass-a-volleyball/) explains the platform and the footwork, and the [wall passing drill](/drills/wall-passing/) and [pepper](/drills/pepper-volleyball-drill/) give you ways to practise.

## How it compares

- **Against the [Tandem Set Rite](/gear/training-equipment/tandem-set-rite-setting-trainer/):** the same idea for a different skill. The Set Rite stops you from palming the ball when you set.
- **Against a [rebounder net](/gear/training-equipment/apex-volleyball-rebounder-net/):** a rebounder gives you repetitions alone. The Pass Rite corrects technique. They work well together.

The cuffs are small and light on purpose: the maker designed them so they do not get in the way where the ball meets your arms.

## Good to know

The price for this simple band ranges from under $30 to about $40 depending on the store, so compare before you buy. Check the hook-and-loop cuffs from time to time. They are the part that wears out first.
