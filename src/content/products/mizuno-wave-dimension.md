## Who it is for

Not every player needs a flagship shoe, and not every player should stay in an entry-level one. The Wave Dimension sits between the two.

Mizuno built it for stability and comfort. The heel uses what Mizuno calls Foam Wave, filled with ENERZY foam, so landings feel soft. The sole has an outrigger on both sides, which makes the base wider. A stabilizer under the midfoot stops the shoe from twisting when you plant and change direction.

That mix suits players who train two or three times a week and want a shoe that feels safe: middle blockers who land again and again, taller or heavier players, and anyone moving up from a basic shoe.

## Who should skip it

- **Players who want speed first.** The [Mizuno Wave Lightning Z8](/gear/shoes/mizuno-wave-lightning-z8/) is lighter and has bouncier foam under the forefoot.
- **Complete beginners who play once a week.** A cheaper shoe such as the [ASICS Gel-Rocket 12](/gear/shoes/asics-gel-rocket-12/) or the [adidas Ligra 8](/gear/shoes/adidas-ligra-8/) is enough.
- **Advanced hitters.** If you jump a lot at a high level, the [Wave Momentum 3](/gear/shoes/mizuno-wave-momentum-3/) gives you more for the extra money.

## Sizing and fit

The Wave Dimension comes as a low-top and as a Mid with a higher collar. A higher collar does not replace an ankle brace, but some players like the feel. We explain the difference in [low-top vs mid-top volleyball shoes](/guides/low-top-vs-mid-top-volleyball-shoes/).

Fit advice is not consistent between stores. Road Runner Sports recommends sizing up half a size if you are between sizes, and Mizuno shoes have a reputation for a close fit. Buy from a store with free returns, or try them on in person.

## How it compares

- **Against the [Mizuno Wave Luminous 3](/gear/shoes/mizuno-wave-luminous-3/):** the Luminous is the comfortable all-rounder with newer ENERZY NXT foam. The Dimension is the more stable of the two.
- **Against the [ASICS Gel-Tactic 12](/gear/shoes/asics-gel-tactic-12/):** similar price, similar goal. The Gel-Tactic uses GEL cushioning and a TRUSSTIC part for midfoot stability. Pick the brand that fits your foot.
- **Against the [Wave Momentum 3](/gear/shoes/mizuno-wave-momentum-3/):** same idea, higher level, higher price.

## Good to know

Mizuno's Intercool system puts ventilation channels in the sole to let heat and moisture out. It will not keep your feet dry in a hot gym, but it helps the shoe dry between sessions.
