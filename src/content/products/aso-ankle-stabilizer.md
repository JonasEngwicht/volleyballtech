## Who it is for

The ASO is the brace many trainers reach for first. Its figure-eight straps work against rolling the ankle inward or outward, which is the movement behind most volleyball sprains.

Players who have rolled an ankle before, or who just want sturdy protection, often choose it.

## Sizing

Measure around the heel and the top of the foot, then compare with the maker's chart. A brace that is too loose does not do its job, and one that is too tight will cut off circulation.

## Who should skip it

- **Players who want the lightest, thinnest option.** The [McDavid 195](/gear/ankle-braces/mcdavid-195-ankle-brace/) is lighter and often cheaper.
- **Anyone who needs a rigid brace.** For a serious injury, ask a medical professional.
