## Who it is for

The Wave Lightning has been Mizuno's speed shoe for many years, and the Z8 keeps that job. At about 295 g it is light enough that you stop noticing it, which is exactly what quick outside hitters, setters and defenders want.

The update is under the forefoot. Mizuno added ENERZY NXT, a nitrogen-infused foam, on top of the regular ENERZY midsole, plus an ENERZY sockliner. The idea is a softer landing and more spring on take-off without extra weight. Mizuno says the Z8 weighs the same as the Z7 while cushioning better.

The new Wave plate runs further along the outside of the forefoot. That is the part of the shoe you push against when you plant for an approach or shuffle sideways on defense.

## Who should skip it

- **Beginners.** You pay for materials you will not feel yet. Start with the [ASICS Gel-Rocket 12](/gear/shoes/asics-gel-rocket-12/) or the [adidas Ligra 8](/gear/shoes/adidas-ligra-8/).
- **Heavy jumpers with knee or shin pain.** A thicker, more cushioned shoe such as the [ASICS Sky Elite FF 4](/gear/shoes/asics-sky-elite-ff-4/) takes more out of each landing.
- **Players who want a tough upper.** The mesh is thin on purpose. If you get stepped on a lot at the net, a shoe with more overlays will last longer.

## Sizing and fit

The upper has no stitched seams, and the eyelets wrap around the midfoot, so the shoe is meant to sit close to the foot. That suits narrow and medium feet. If your feet are wide, look for the wide version, or try the shoe on before you commit.

The heel is rounded. Mizuno designed it for the heel-to-toe roll at the start of a spike approach, so do not be surprised if the shoe feels slightly rocker-like when you walk.

Wear your game socks when you try them on. If you play in [ankle braces](/gear/ankle-braces/), bring those too, because a brace changes the fit of a slim shoe more than a roomy one.

## How it compares

- **Against the [Mizuno Wave Momentum 3](/gear/shoes/mizuno-wave-momentum-3/):** the Momentum is the stable all-position shoe and weighs more. The Lightning is for players who would trade some support for speed.
- **Against the [ASICS Netburner Ballistic FF 4](/gear/shoes/asics-netburner-ballistic-ff-4/):** both are speed shoes. The Netburner has a higher heel drop of 15 mm; the Lightning sits a little flatter at about 11 mm.
- **Against the [adidas Crazyflight 7](/gear/shoes/adidas-crazyflight-7/):** similar weight class, different foam. Fit decides this one, so try both if you can.

## Good to know

The monofilament mesh is see-through in places, so dark socks will show. Mizuno also says the upper uses recycled material: more than half of the synthetic leather base fabric and most of the laces.

Like every indoor shoe, the Lightning Z8 keeps its grip longest if you wear it only on the court. See [how to clean volleyball shoes](/guides/how-to-clean-volleyball-shoes/).
