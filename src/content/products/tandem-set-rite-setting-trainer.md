## Who it is for

A common beginner setting mistake is letting the ball land in the palm. The Set Rite has a small knob that sits on your palm, so you feel it when the ball touches the wrong place.

Use it for a few sessions to build the habit, then take it off.

## Try it with a drill

Wear the straps and do the [wall setting drill](/drills/wall-setting/). If the knob touches the ball, your hands are too low or too flat.

## Who should skip it

- **Experienced setters** who already have clean hands.
- **Players who need to work on footwork first.** See the [approach and footwork drill](/drills/approach-footwork/).
