## Who it is for

The Team knee pad suits players who spend a lot of time on the floor. The foam is split into several sections, which lets the pad bend with your knee while still protecting its front and sides.

That makes it a good match for liberos, defensive specialists and beginners who have not yet learned to dive with control.

## Who should skip it

- **Hitters and middle blockers** who rarely dive may find it more padding than they need. See the [Mizuno LR6](/gear/knee-pads/mizuno-lr6-knee-pads/) for a slimmer option.
- **Players who want the shortest, lightest pad possible.**

## Care

Wash by hand or on a gentle cycle in cold water and let the pads air-dry. Our [knee pad guide](/guides/how-to-choose-volleyball-knee-pads/) has more care tips.
