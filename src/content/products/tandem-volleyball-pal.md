## Who it is for

The slow part of practising alone is not the hitting. It is the walking. You serve one ball, walk to get it, walk back, and serve again. In ten minutes you may get fifteen serves.

The Volleyball Pal removes the walking. The ball sits in a holder on an elastic cord, and the cord guides it back to you after every hit. The maker describes it as a tool for practising serving tosses and arm swing technique.

That makes it useful for:

- **New players** who are building the serving motion for the first time
- **Players without a net or a partner**
- **Anyone short on space**, since the ball never travels far

It costs between $24 and $30 at the big sporting goods stores, which puts it among the cheapest training tools you can buy.

## Who should skip it

- **Players who already serve consistently.** You need targets and distance now. Use our [serving targets drill](/drills/serving-targets/).
- **Players who want to practise passing or setting.** A [rebounder net](/gear/training-equipment/apex-volleyball-rebounder-net/) sends the ball back in a more natural arc.
- **Teams.** This is a one-player tool.

## How to use it well

A returning ball tempts you to hit as fast as you can. Do the opposite.

1. **Start with the toss only.** Toss, let the ball drop, catch it. The cord makes a bad toss obvious.
2. **Add the arm swing at half speed.** High elbow, open hand, contact in the middle of the ball.
3. **Stop the ball between hits.** Catch it, reset your feet, and go again. Ten careful serves beat fifty rushed ones.

Then take the motion to a real court. Our guide on [how to serve a volleyball](/learn/how-to-serve-a-volleyball/) walks through the underhand, the standing float and the jump serve step by step.

## How it compares

- **Against a [rebounder net](/gear/training-equipment/apex-volleyball-rebounder-net/):** the rebounder is bigger, costs more and handles passing, setting and hitting. The Volleyball Pal is small, cheap and mostly about the serve and arm swing.
- **Against the [Tandem Pass Rite](/gear/training-equipment/tandem-pass-rite/):** different skill. The Pass Rite trains quiet arms in the forearm pass.
- **Against a [portable net](/gear/training-equipment/park-sun-spiker-sport-net/):** a net lets you see where the ball lands, which no cord trainer can.

## Good to know

Listings differ on what is in the box, so check whether a ball is included and how the cord attaches before you order. An elastic cord under tension can snap back. Keep other people, especially small children, out of the area in front of you while you practise.
