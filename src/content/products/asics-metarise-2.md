## Who it is for

The Metarise 2 is a specialist. ASICS designed it to help outside and opposite hitters turn a fast approach into height: the curved toe and stiff plate work together when you plant and jump.

That makes it a shoe for experienced hitters with a consistent approach who train several times a week.

## Who should skip it

- **Anyone still learning their approach.** A stiff, curved sole rewards good technique and does little for you without it. Start with our [approach footwork drill](/drills/approach-footwork/).
- **Setters, liberos and all-around players.** You will be better served by a more flexible shoe.
- **Anyone on a budget.** The [Sky Elite FF 4](/gear/shoes/asics-sky-elite-ff-4/) covers most hitters for less. See our [Sky Elite vs. Metarise comparison](/compare/asics-sky-elite-ff-4-vs-metarise-2/).
