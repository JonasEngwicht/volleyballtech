## Who it is for

Some shoes are built for one job. The Wave Luminous 3 is built for all of them, which makes it a good pick for players who rotate through the back row and the front row, or who have not settled on a position yet.

Mizuno says the third version "makes new strides in comfort". In practice that means ENERZY NXT foam in the midsole, a Wave plate that spreads the force of a landing over a wider area, and a woven upper that feels soft against the foot.

At about 305 g it is only a little heavier than the [Wave Lightning Z8](/gear/shoes/mizuno-wave-lightning-z8/), and it costs less.

## Who should skip it

- **Players who want maximum stability.** The [Wave Momentum 3](/gear/shoes/mizuno-wave-momentum-3/) and the [Wave Dimension](/gear/shoes/mizuno-wave-dimension/) have a wider, firmer base.
- **Players who want the lightest shoe.** That is the [Wave Lightning Z8](/gear/shoes/mizuno-wave-lightning-z8/).
- **Beginners on a tight budget.** See our [best volleyball shoes for beginners](/best/best-volleyball-shoes-for-beginners/).

## Sizing and fit

The woven upper is the reason many players choose this shoe. It is soft, it bends with the foot, and it is unlikely to rub while it is new. The trade-off is hold: a soft upper gives a little more than a stiff one when you cut hard, so lace it firmly.

Mizuno also fits a cup insole, a footbed shaped to cradle the heel. If you use your own insoles, check that they sit flat when you swap them in.

As with other Mizuno shoes, expect a close fit, and try them on with your game socks.

## How it compares

- **Against the [Nike HyperAce 3](/gear/shoes/nike-hyperace-3/):** both are all-round shoes at a similar price. The Nike uses Air Zoom cushioning, the Mizuno uses foam and a plate. Fit is the deciding factor.
- **Against the [Mizuno Wave Momentum 3](/gear/shoes/mizuno-wave-momentum-3/):** the Momentum is the step up for players who jump a lot and want more support.
- **Against the [ASICS Gel-Rocket 12](/gear/shoes/asics-gel-rocket-12/):** the Gel-Rocket is cheaper and simpler. The Luminous gives you newer foam and a softer upper.

## Good to know

XG Rubber is Mizuno's high-grip outsole compound. Like all soft court rubber it collects dust, so wipe the soles with a damp cloth when the floor is dirty. More tips are in [how to clean volleyball shoes](/guides/how-to-clean-volleyball-shoes/).
