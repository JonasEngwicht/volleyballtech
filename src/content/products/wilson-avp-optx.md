## Who it is for

Beach volleyball uses a larger, softer ball than the indoor game, and the OPTX is built for it. The graphics are the main feature: the pattern makes it easier to see the ball's spin as it flies.

It makes sense for committed beach players and doubles teams who want the tour ball.

## Who should skip it

- **Casual beach players.** A cheaper outdoor ball is plenty for pickup games.
- **Indoor players.** Choose an indoor ball such as the [Mikasa V200W](/gear/balls/mikasa-v200w/) or the [Molten V5M5000](/gear/balls/molten-v5m5000/).

Learn more about the differences in our [indoor vs. beach volleyball guide](/guides/indoor-vs-beach-volleyballs/).
