## Who it is for

ASICS sorts its volleyball shoes by playing style, and the Netburner Ballistic is the "faster" one. Everything about it is aimed at acceleration and direction changes: a flexible sole, a light upper and a raised heel that keeps you leaning forward and ready to move.

That fits setters and back-row players, who take far more quick steps than big jumps.

## Who should skip it

- **Hitters and middles who jump constantly.** You will want more cushioning under the forefoot. See the [Sky Elite FF 4](/gear/shoes/asics-sky-elite-ff-4/).
- **Players who like a flat, low shoe.** The 15 mm drop gives a noticeably raised heel.
