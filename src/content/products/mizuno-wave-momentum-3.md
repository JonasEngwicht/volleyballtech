## Who it is for

If you play more than one position, the Wave Momentum 3 is an easy shoe to recommend. It is cushioned enough for hitters, stable enough for blockers and not so heavy that a setter will hate it.

The Wave plate is Mizuno's signature idea: a shaped plate in the heel that spreads impact sideways when you land.

## Who should skip it

- **Players chasing the lightest possible shoe.** Look at the [adidas Crazyflight 7](/gear/shoes/adidas-crazyflight-7/) or the [Netburner Ballistic FF 4](/gear/shoes/asics-netburner-ballistic-ff-4/).
- **Beginners on a budget.** The [Gel-Rocket 12](/gear/shoes/asics-gel-rocket-12/) costs far less.

Fit reports differ by region and by men's, women's and unisex versions. Check Mizuno's size chart for the exact version you are buying, and order from a shop with easy returns.
