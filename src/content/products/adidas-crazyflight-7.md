## Who it is for

adidas rebuilt the Crazyflight for its seventh version. The main change is the midsole: a full-length layer of Lightstrike Pro foam for softer landings and more energy return, on top of a second foam layer for stability.

The **low** version suits players who want speed and free ankle movement. The **mid** adds a reinforced collar for hitters and blockers who land in traffic at the net.

## Who should skip it

- **Beginners.** adidas' own entry-level court shoes cost far less.
- **Players who rely on a very structured, rigid shoe.** The Crazyflight is on the light and flexible side.
