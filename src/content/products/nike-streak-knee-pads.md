## Who it is for

Walk into any American school gym and you will see the Nike swoosh on a lot of knees. The Streak is Nike's standard volleyball pad, and it has been for years.

The padding is EVA foam, the same family of material used in shoe midsoles. It is shaped to curve around the knee instead of sitting on it like a flat block. The sleeve is a Dri-FIT knit that moves sweat away from the skin, with thinner vent zones behind the knee where pads usually get hot. Inside, the fabric is brushed so it feels soft.

It is an all-round pad: enough foam for normal dives and slides, slim enough that hitters do not feel slowed down.

## Who should skip it

- **Players with muscular or thick legs.** The fit is slim. If the largest size is still tight, try the [McDavid HEX knee pads](/gear/knee-pads/mcdavid-hex-knee-pads/), which come in six sizes.
- **Bargain hunters.** The [Mizuno LR6](/gear/knee-pads/mizuno-lr6-knee-pads/) and the [Mizuno T10 Plus](/gear/knee-pads/mizuno-t10-plus-knee-pads/) usually cost less.
- **Players who want side protection.** The [Mizuno Team](/gear/knee-pads/mizuno-team-knee-pads/) wraps foam around the sides of the joint.

## Sizing and fit

This is the part to get right. Nike sells four adult sizes, and the fit is slim. Reviews on Nike and Dick's Sporting Goods repeat the same point: the pads feel tight, and people with bigger legs should go up a size.

A good knee pad stays put when you bend your knee fully and when you slide. It should not leave your lower leg tingling. New pads loosen slightly after a few sessions, so a pad that is snug on day one is usually right.

## How it compares

- **Against the [Mizuno LR6](/gear/knee-pads/mizuno-lr6-knee-pads/):** the LR6 is the classic low-profile pad with a short 6-inch sleeve. It is usually cheaper. The Streak has a softer lining and Nike's vent zones.
- **Against the [Mizuno T10 Plus](/gear/knee-pads/mizuno-t10-plus-knee-pads/):** the T10 Plus has a longer sleeve and comes in one adult size. The Streak gives you a choice of sizes.
- **Against the [McDavid HEX](/gear/knee-pads/mcdavid-hex-knee-pads/):** the HEX is a thin compression sleeve with flexible padding. It protects against scrapes and light knocks more than hard landings.

## Good to know

Each pad has a small ID tag where you can write your name or number. On a team where everyone wears the same black pads, that is more useful than it sounds.

Not every buyer is happy with how long the pads last. A few reviews describe a hole worn through the padding in one season. Players who slide a lot on rough floors should expect to replace pads every season, whatever the brand.
