## Who it is for

If your club or school plays with Mikasa balls, the V200W is the one to practice with at home. It feels like the match ball and behaves the same way.

It is designed for indoor courts. Players who train seriously and want a consistent ball will notice the difference compared with cheaper balls.

## Who should skip it

- **Beginners and casual players.** A cheaper ball does the same job at your level.
- **Outdoor players.** Pick a dedicated beach ball such as the [Wilson AVP OPTX](/gear/balls/wilson-avp-optx/).
