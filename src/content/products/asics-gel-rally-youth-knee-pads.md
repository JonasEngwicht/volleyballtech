## Who it is for

Young players fall a lot, and a knee that hurts after every dive is a quick way to lose interest. The Gel-Rally youth pad is an inexpensive way to protect a child's knees during a first season.

Because it is sized for smaller legs, it stays in place better than an adult pad that has been pushed up or stretched.

## What to check before buying

1. Measure around the center of the kneecap with the leg straight.
2. Compare it with the size chart on the listing.
3. Make sure the padding sits over the kneecap and the bone just below it.

If your child is older or bigger, look at adult pads like the [Mizuno LR6](/gear/knee-pads/mizuno-lr6-knee-pads/). For general advice, read how to [choose volleyball knee pads](/guides/how-to-choose-volleyball-knee-pads/).
