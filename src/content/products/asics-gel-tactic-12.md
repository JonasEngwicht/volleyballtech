## Who it is for

ASICS describes the Gel-Tactic 12 as a stable and supportive shoe for court players who want a balance of flexibility and stability. It is the middle rung of the ASICS ladder: above the [Upcourt 6](/gear/shoes/asics-upcourt-6/) and the [Gel-Rocket 12](/gear/shoes/asics-gel-rocket-12/), below the [Netburner Ballistic FF 4](/gear/shoes/asics-netburner-ballistic-ff-4/).

Three things set it apart from the cheaper models:

1. **An outrigger.** The outsole wraps up the outer edge of the shoe. That gives you a wider base when you push off sideways.
2. **Printed reinforcements.** Thin printed layers on the outer side work with the laces to hold the foot in place.
3. **A TRUSSTIC part on the outer midsole.** This hard plastic piece stops the shoe from twisting under the arch.

The forefoot uses soft mesh, so the shoe bends easily where your foot bends.

## Who should skip it

- **Hitters who jump a lot.** GEL is fine for everyday play, but the foams in the [Sky Elite FF 4](/gear/shoes/asics-sky-elite-ff-4/) absorb far more.
- **Players with a heavy schedule.** If you practise four times a week and play tournaments, buy a shoe from the next level up. It costs more and lasts longer.
- **Anyone who finds the Gel-Rocket 12 on sale and plays once a week.** The cheaper shoe will do.

## Sizing and fit

Buyers on Zappos rate the Gel-Tactic 12 true to size and true to width, with moderate arch support. ASICS sells wide versions in some regions, so check the width when you order.

The insole comes out. That is useful if you wear orthotics or simply want to dry the shoes faster after practice.

## How it compares

- **Against the [ASICS Gel-Rocket 12](/gear/shoes/asics-gel-rocket-12/):** a little more support for about $20 more at list price. We compare the two cheapest ASICS shoes in [Gel-Rocket 12 vs. Upcourt 6](/compare/asics-gel-rocket-12-vs-upcourt-6/).
- **Against the [Mizuno Wave Dimension](/gear/shoes/mizuno-wave-dimension/):** the closest rival. The Mizuno has softer heel foam and a mid-top option.
- **Against the [adidas Ligra 8](/gear/shoes/adidas-ligra-8/):** the Ligra is cheaper and simpler. The Gel-Tactic has more stability parts.

## Good to know

The list price is $100, but this model is discounted often. ASICS itself has listed some colors at about $60. At that price it is one of the better deals for a school season.
