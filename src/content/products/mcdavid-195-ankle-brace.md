## Who it is for

The McDavid 195 is one of the most common lace-up braces in gyms. The laces hold the ankle snugly and the crossing straps imitate the figure-6 wrap athletic trainers use with tape.

It is a good fit for players who want protection against rolls at the net but do not need a rigid brace.

## Manufacturer claims

McDavid says an independent university study found athletes wearing the 195 had three times fewer ankle injuries. We could not check the study ourselves, so treat the figure as a marketing claim.

## Who should skip it

- **Anyone recovering from a serious sprain.** Ask a doctor or physical therapist what level of support you need. A [rigid brace like the Zamst A2-DX](/gear/ankle-braces/zamst-a2-dx-ankle-brace/) offers more control.
- **Players who hate fiddling with laces.**
