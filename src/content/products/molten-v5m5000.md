## Who it is for

The V5M5000 is a competition ball. If your league uses Molten, practicing with the same ball makes passing and serving feel familiar on match day.

Molten's patented FLISTATEC design uses a pattern of hexagonal panels to cut turbulence around the ball.

## Who should skip it

- **Beginners.** A less expensive training ball is fine while you learn.
- **Backyard or beach play.** Use a ball made for the outdoors, such as the [Wilson AVP OPTX](/gear/balls/wilson-avp-optx/).
