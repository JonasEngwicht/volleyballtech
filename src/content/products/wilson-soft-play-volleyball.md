## Who it is for

Not every volleyball needs to be an official match ball. Sometimes you just want something to pass around at a barbecue, take to the beach, or give to a child who has discovered the sport.

The Wilson Soft Play is made for that. Under the synthetic leather cover sits a thin layer of sponge. It takes the sting out of the ball, which matters more than experienced players remember. Beginners who are afraid of the ball pull their arms away. Beginners who are not afraid learn to pass.

The ball is official size and weight. That is the important detail. Light beach toys and foam balls are fun, but they float and bounce differently, so nothing you learn with them carries over. With the Soft Play, your platform and your timing are the same ones you will need with a real ball.

Good uses:

- backyard and park games
- holidays at the beach or lake
- a first ball for a teenager
- extra balls for PE classes and summer camps

## Who should skip it

- **Players on a team.** Buy the ball your team uses for practice at home: for many high schools that is a ball like the [Tachikara SV-5WSC](/gear/balls/tachikara-sv5wsc/).
- **Serious beach players.** The [Wilson AVP OPTX](/gear/balls/wilson-avp-optx/) and the [Mikasa BV550C](/gear/balls/mikasa-bv550c-beach-pro/) are in a different class, at a different price.
- **Children under about 12.** A full-weight ball is heavy for small arms. See the youth options in our [volleyball size guide](/guides/volleyball-size-guide/).

## Size and feel

"Official size" means the standard adult ball, roughly 65 to 67 cm around. What differs from a match ball is the construction. The 18 panels are sewn by machine rather than bonded to the ball, and the cover is thicker and softer.

In play, that means the ball is a little less lively and a little less exact. For learning the forearm pass, the overhead pass and the serve, that is no problem at all. Try our [wall passing drill](/drills/wall-passing/) with it.

## How it compares

- **Against a composite indoor ball** such as the [Tachikara SV-5WSC](/gear/balls/tachikara-sv5wsc/): the indoor ball is more precise and is approved for matches. It also costs two to three times as much and should not be used on rough ground.
- **Against the [Wilson AVP OPTX](/gear/balls/wilson-avp-optx/):** the OPTX is a professional beach ball. The Soft Play is a recreational ball that happens to be fine on sand.

Our guide to [indoor vs. beach volleyballs](/guides/indoor-vs-beach-volleyballs/) explains which ball belongs on which surface, and the [best volleyballs](/best/best-volleyballs/) lists our picks for each use.

## Good to know

The ball usually arrives flat or soft. Pump it until it gives slightly when you press with both thumbs, and moisten the needle first.

Sewn balls soak up water through the seams. Do not leave the ball out overnight, and let it dry before you store it. Treated that way, a cheap ball easily lasts several summers.
