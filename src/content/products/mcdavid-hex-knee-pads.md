## Who it is for

Most volleyball knee pads are a block of foam in a sleeve. The McDavid HEX works differently. It is a compression sleeve with dozens of small foam hexagons bonded to the front.

That design comes from basketball and football, where players want padding that does not slow them down. In volleyball it suits:

- **Hitters and middles** who rarely hit the floor but want cover for the times they do
- **Setters** who slide more than they dive
- **Players with large legs**, because there are six adult sizes
- **Athletes who play several sports** and want one pad for all of them

The sleeve fabric is 80% nylon and 20% spandex. It squeezes the leg lightly, which is what keeps the pad in place.

## Who should skip it

- **Liberos and defensive specialists.** Nine millimeters of flexible foam is not much when your knee meets the floor twenty times a match. Look at the [Mizuno Team knee pads](/gear/knee-pads/mizuno-team-knee-pads/).
- **Beginners who fall often.** A thicker and cheaper pad such as the [Mizuno T10 Plus](/gear/knee-pads/mizuno-t10-plus-knee-pads/) forgives more.
- **Players who run hot.** Several buyers describe the sleeve as warm.

## Sizing and fit

Because this is a compression sleeve, size matters more than with a loose pad. McDavid's method: stand up, bend the leg a little, and measure around the knee over the kneecap. The youth size (6440Y) covers 10 to 13 inches.

If you are between sizes, think about how you will use it. A tighter sleeve stays put better. A looser one is more comfortable for long tournaments.

## How it compares

- **Against the [Mizuno LR6](/gear/knee-pads/mizuno-lr6-knee-pads/):** the LR6 is the slim option among classic volleyball pads and has thicker padding in the landing zone. The HEX is thinner still and covers a longer part of the leg.
- **Against the [Nike Streak](/gear/knee-pads/nike-streak-knee-pads/):** the Streak has more foam. The HEX has more sizes and can be machine dried.

## Good to know

McDavid sells the same sleeve for elbows and shins, which is why listings call it a "knee/elbow/shin pad". Make sure you order by knee measurement, and check whether the listing is for a pair. The 6440 is sold as a pair; some other McDavid sleeves are sold singly.
