## Who it is for

The Sky Elite line is ASICS' shoe for players who spend the match in the air. ASICS says the fourth version is built to support higher jumps and more stable landings, with a midsole that is meant to feel soft on impact and springy on take-off.

Pick the regular low-top if you want free ankle movement or wear ankle braces. Pick the MT if you like a higher collar.

## Who should skip it

- **Beginners and casual players.** You will not feel most of what you are paying for. Look at the [Gel-Rocket 12](/gear/shoes/asics-gel-rocket-12/) instead.
- **Liberos and defensive specialists** who want a low, fast shoe. The [Netburner Ballistic FF 4](/gear/shoes/asics-netburner-ballistic-ff-4/) is built for that.

## Good to know

The previous Sky Elite FF 3 is still sold in many shops, often at a clear discount. If the price of the new model puts you off, the older one is worth a look.
