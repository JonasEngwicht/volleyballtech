## Who it is for

The Ligra 8 answers a simple question: what is the cheapest shoe I can wear to volleyball practice without playing in running shoes?

It does the basics. The rubber sole is made for indoor floors and will not leave marks. The shoe sits low and flat, so you feel steadier when you move sideways than in a tall, soft running shoe. The mesh upper lets air in, and synthetic leather overlays give it some shape.

That makes it a good match for:

- beginners in their first season
- middle school and high school players who also use the shoe for PE
- adults in a casual weekly league
- kids, who will outgrow any shoe within a year

## Who should skip it

- **Players who jump a lot.** There is no special foam or plate here. Hitters and middles should look at the shoes in our [best volleyball shoes for jumping](/best/best-volleyball-shoes-for-jumping/) list.
- **Club players.** For several practices a week, step up to the [ASICS Gel-Rocket 12](/gear/shoes/asics-gel-rocket-12/) at least, or to the [ASICS Gel-Tactic 12](/gear/shoes/asics-gel-tactic-12/) or the [Mizuno Wave Dimension](/gear/shoes/mizuno-wave-dimension/).
- **Players who need a narrow, locked-in fit.** The regular fit is comfortable but not tight.

## Sizing and fit

adidas rates the Ligra 8 true to size and recommends your usual size, and buyers on the adidas site mostly agree. The fit is regular, which helps if your feet are a bit wide or if you wear thick socks.

If you plan to wear [ankle braces](/gear/ankle-braces/), a roomier shoe like this one makes it easier to fit a lace-up brace inside.

## How it compares

- **Against the [ASICS Upcourt 6](/gear/shoes/asics-upcourt-6/):** the closest rival at a similar price. Both are light, basic court shoes with kids' versions. Buy whichever fits your foot better.
- **Against the [ASICS Gel-Rocket 12](/gear/shoes/asics-gel-rocket-12/):** the Gel-Rocket costs a little more and adds GEL cushioning and a stability part under the arch. It is the better choice if you already know you will keep playing.

New to the sport? Our guides on [what to wear for volleyball](/guides/what-to-wear-for-volleyball/) and the [volleyball gear checklist](/guides/volleyball-gear-checklist/) cover the rest of your bag.

## Good to know

Keep court shoes for the court. If you wear the Ligra 8 outside, the soft rubber picks up dirt and wears down, and you lose the grip you bought it for. Carry the shoes to the gym and change there.
