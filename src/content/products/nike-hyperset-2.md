## Who it is for

The HyperSet 2 is the Nike shoe for front-row players. The Air Zoom unit sits under the ball of the foot, right where you push off, and the strap system holds the midfoot tight when you plant and land.

The independent testers at WearTesters rated it highly for traction, lockdown and cushioning, and found it true to size.

## Who should skip it

- **Players with wide feet.** Reviewers describe the fit as fairly narrow. Try it on, or consider going half a size up.
- **Anyone who plays in dusty gyms and hates wiping soles.**
- **Budget buyers.** The [HyperAce 3](/gear/shoes/nike-hyperace-3/) costs less.

## Editions

Nike sells the shoe in a standard version and in "SE" special editions with different colors and prices. The shoe underneath is the same.
